# Nonviolent Main Outcome

Kindred's first vertical slice must include at least one legitimate main outcome where the player can resolve the local crisis, receive final XP, reach level 2, and choose a first improvement without defeating any enemy. This is intentional even though the game has combat and XP: the slice must prove that observation, dialogue, positioning, escape, and understanding ecosystem chain effects can be a complete progression path, not merely optional flavor around combat.

Defeating enemies may still be a valid path, and the nonviolent path may still include danger, self-defense, stealth, and retreat. The boundary is that no enemy defeat may be required for at least one legitimate crisis outcome.
